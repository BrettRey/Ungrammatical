# Literature survey: Mendeley library.bib

Source file: `/Users/brettreynolds/Documents/Mendeley Desktop/library.bib`
(1820 entries, last touched 2017-03-09).

The library is a 2017-era export, so many of the named figures in the
restructure plan are simply not in it: Hebdige, Polhemus, Lippi-Green,
Deborah Cameron, Bergen on swearing, Sandler, Bopp, Henry Sweet,
Khalidi, Slater, Ereshefsky, Reydon, P.D. Magnus, Rachell Powell,
Brigandt, Schwitzgebel, Schindler, Dennett, Caxton, Futrell, Mahowald,
the original Hankamer-Postal *whose* paper, the Cinque adverbial
hierarchy. Those will need to be sourced elsewhere. What follows is what
the Mendeley library does cover that maps onto the new chapter list,
keyed by best-fit chapter.

## Ch 1: The road is long long

The library has nothing on reduplication or intensification specifically.
What it does have, on contact features and dialect emergence, fits
better in ch 9 or ch 10 (see those sections), but a few items could
support the long-long opening if they're folded into the framing.

- `@Trudgill2010` -- Trudgill 2010 -- "Contact and Sociolinguistic
  Typology" -- argues low-contact varieties grow morphological complexity;
  useful framing for dialect features that look ungrammatical to outsiders.
- `@Aikhenvald2003` -- Aikhenvald 2003 -- "Mechanisms of change in areal
  diffusion: new morphology and language contact" -- mechanisms of
  contact-driven grammaticalization in multilingual settings.
