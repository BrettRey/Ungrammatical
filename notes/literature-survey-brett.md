# Literature survey: Brett's own writing

Maps Brett's portfolio papers and existing book chapters to the 15-chapter
restructure. Goal: identify what Brett-authored material can feed each new
chapter. Paths are absolute. Existing-chapter notes flag the load-bearing
original work to lift, not the broader ungainly text.

Conventions:
- "papers/<folder>/" = a portfolio paper Brett authored or co-authored.
- "chapters/<file>" = existing book chapter (Brett-authored throughout).
- "Lift" = preserve as set piece. "Source" = quarry for arguments/data.

## Ch 13: What grammaticality is

NEW synthesis chapter. HPC theory: Boyd, Khalidi, Slater, Ereshefsky,
Reydon, Magnus, Powell. The cluster account named.

This chapter has the heaviest write but also the most Brett-source
material. Almost the whole HPC programme feeds it.

- `papers/Grammaticality_as_Kind_Miller/main.tex` — **the central
  source**. The Reynolds-Miller paper argues grammaticality is an HPC
  kind: morphosyntactic well-formedness, semantic compatibility,
  community entrenchment as the cluster; child-acquisition / repair-
  strategies / sociolinguistic-policing as homeostatic mechanisms.
  Two-level distinction (objective grammaticality vs subjective
  feeling). System-level claim (Claim B) that grammaticality as a
