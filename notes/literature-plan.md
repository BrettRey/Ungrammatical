# Literature plan — *(Un)grammatical*

A consolidated per-chapter map across four sources: Brett's portfolio, the
HPC book, the central `literature/` folder, and the Mendeley `library.bib`
(2017 export). Each chapter section lists what's already cited in
`localbibliography.bib`, what Brett-authored material to lift, what the HPC
book provides as academic backbone, what's available in `literature/`, what
the Mendeley library can supply, and what gaps need external sourcing.

The book runs to 15 chapters per `notes/restructure-plan.md`. The synthesis
chapter (ch 13) is the heaviest write; ch 9 (whose grammar) and ch 14
(getting wrong) need most net new sourcing; chs 1, 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 12 are
already well-supplied between Brett's own draft material and the surveys.
