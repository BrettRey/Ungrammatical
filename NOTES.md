## Connections to other portfolio projects

The book is the trade-style outlet for Brett's HPC work on
grammaticality. Likely points of integration:

- `papers/Grammaticality_de_idealized/` — the OVMG model and the
  "absolute blocks" treatment now resolved by the LBE paper
- `papers/Grammaticality_as_Kind_Miller/` — joint paper engaging the
  "zone of max systematicity" framing
- `papers/Grammaticality_judgments_as_detectors/` — what the
  ungrammaticality detector is
- `papers/Field_relative_HPC_categories/` — field-relative
  projectibility framing
- `papers/Functions_as_Comparanda__Categories_as_Kinds__A_Homeostatic_Approach_to_Typology/`
  — the typological backbone

When drafting chapters that draw on these papers, cite the academic
versions in `localbibliography.bib` rather than copying prose
directly; the book is for a wider readership and the paper prose will
usually need translating.
