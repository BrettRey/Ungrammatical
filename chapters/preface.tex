 The * is used by statisticians to mark a sentence as ungrammatical. Its origins trace back to philologists who used it to signify a word that, although unattested in ancient languages, was suggested by evidence to exist. It meant that no actual instance of such a word could be found in any source. Generative grammarians subsequently intensified the meaning of *, shifting it from `unattested' to `impossible'. (I learned this from Chris Potts.)
