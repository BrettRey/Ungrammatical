% EDITORIAL-HPC: "Impossible" is rhetorically useful, but later chapters need
% to qualify it: generative usage made the asterisk look like a verdict on
% formal possibility, while the book's final account treats it as a fallible
% detector signal calibrated to a community and a construction.
 The * is used by linguists to mark a sentence as ungrammatical. Its origins trace back to philologists who used it to signify a word that, although unattested in ancient languages, was suggested by evidence to exist. It meant that no actual instance of such a word could be found in any source. Generative grammarians subsequently intensified the meaning of *, shifting it from `unattested' to `impossible'. (I learned this from Chris Potts.)
