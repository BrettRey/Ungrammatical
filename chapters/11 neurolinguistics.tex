\chapter{Seeing grammar in the brain}

\section{Grammar errors are stressful}

When our minds work, our pupils swell.
\citet{Just1993} found that working harder to process a sentence led to more pupillary dilation.
Heart rate variability is another measure. \citet{Divjak2024a} found the HRV drops when listeners are exposed to article errors. Drops were larger when L1 accent made the errors and smaller when L2 accent did.
